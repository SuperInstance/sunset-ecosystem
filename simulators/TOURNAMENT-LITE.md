## Emergent Behavior: "The Lighthouse Effect"

**Observation:** The perfect agent (ethos=1.0, pathos=1.0, logos=1.0, fitness=1.0) appears at **generation 16**. Yet the *population mean fitness* continues climbing for another **30+ generations**, rising from 0.80 (gen 16) to 0.94 (gen 49).

**Interpretation:** Once the perfect agent enters the gene pool, it acts as a fixed "lighthouse" — always winning tournaments, always surviving. But because the thermal cap is 30, breeding continuously spawns new offspring that *mutate toward* the lighthouse rather than matching it exactly. The result is a "catch-up swarm": a population of near-perfect agents slowly converging while the champion remains unchanged.

**Key Signature:** Champion fitness flatlines at 1.0 while mean fitness keeps growing. The standard deviations of ethos/pathos/logos shrink but never collapse to zero, indicating mutation maintains a persistent "wake" of sub-perfect agents trailing the lighthouse.

---

## Recommended Breeding Parameter Adjustment

**Current:** Thermal cap fixed at 30.

**Suggested experiment:** **Dynamic thermal cap** — raise cap to 50 after generation 20 (when the lighthouse appears).  
**Rationale:** With more room, the catch-up swarm can maintain higher diversity, potentially exploring alternative paths to perfection and accelerating mean-fitness convergence. A fixed small cap forces premature competition among near-identical agents, wasting breeding cycles on tournaments that all produce the same winner.
